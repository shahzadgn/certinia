8. Strategic Recommendations (Part 1/3)

Immediate Actions
Strategy Review

Evaluate LinkedIn Performance

While LinkedIn campaigns successfully drove platform engagement in February, the lack of downstream CRM leads suggests our current messaging may not be fully resonating with active buyers. This presents a great opportunity to refresh our approach.

👉 Recommendation: We suggest shifting our focus toward testing new ad campaigns and creative ideas. By experimenting with different messaging angles and highlighting new offerings, we can better discover what drives high-quality leads from this channel.

Growth Opportunity

Scale EMEA Budget & Keywords

With Google Ads tracking now fully aligned, the data confirms EMEA is driving highly efficient downstream leads. However, the region's current budget share limits our ability to capture more of this success.

👉 Recommendation: We suggest a calculated increase in EMEA's budget allocation, alongside testing new, high-intent keywords to systematically capture broader regional demand.

Advanced Testing

Pilot PMAX & 6Sense Audiences

To expand our EMEA footprint without sacrificing lead quality, we have an excellent opportunity to test new campaign types and highly targeted account-based marketing (ABM) lists.

👉 Recommendation: Let's pilot a Performance Max (PMAX) campaign integrating 6Sense Audience Segments. We can pair this with fresh ad creatives and newly optimized landing pages to maximize engagement.
